How it works

Create a group

Pick the people and the currency. Japan in ¥? Dinner with friends in S$? Both work.

Add expenses or a receipt

Type an amount, or pick a receipt photo and tap who had what.

Settle up

See who pays whom. Record the payment when it happens.

Features

Groups for any trip

Make a group for a trip abroad (like Japan in ¥) or an everyday group in S$ only.

Live or your own rate

Add expenses in the trip currency. See the ≈ S$ amount using today's rate, or use your own cash rate. Each expense keeps its rate, so later changes never touch past expenses.

Split your way

Split equally, or choose who's in.

Split by receipt

Pick a receipt photo from your library. The app reads it on your phone. Tap who had each item. Service charge and GST are shared in proportion to what each person had.

balances.png

Clear balances

Balances show in one main currency (S$), with suggested payments. They are person to person by default. Turn on "Simplify payments" to use fewer payments and see how it got there.

settle.png

Record payments

Record a payment in S$ or ¥ cash, converted at the receiver's rate. Made a mistake? Tap Undo.

Light and dark mode

Green is the main colour. Orange is the accent.
